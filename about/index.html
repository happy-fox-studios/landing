about us

Here you can find the most important information about our team, as well as the process and the concepts of our work.
The studio was founded in 2021

We are a team of ambitious guys that got tired of working in companies where there wasn't any mutual respect and clear principles. We think that freedom of thought and loving what you do are the perfect conditions where good quality arts can be created and where successful cases are born. That's precisely why we decided to open up our own studio.

how we work
We’ve created a space where artists can work with their favourite projects in a field they like. Everyone on our team can do what inspires them to develop and helps them to get better each day.
We make sure each person feels comfortable on our team by creating conditions where the artist can reach their creative potential. We care about our members' mood and overall state and help to cope with work difficulties.
We are against of showing any sign of aggression and toxic behavior in our team. We openly discuss all the issues and problems that might come up as well as the future of the team. We are sure that quality work can be reached only through the atmosphere of respect and mutual support.
